---
type: glossary
domain: "*"
---

regular_season:
  term: Regular Season
  description: Standard 82-game season (historically varies) excluding playoffs.
playoffs:
  term: Playoffs
  description: Post-season elimination tournament.
pre_season:
  term: Pre-Season
  description: Exhibition games before regular season.
all_star:
  term: All-Star
  description: Annual mid-season exhibition events and games.
finals:
  term: Finals
  description: Championship series between conference winners (best-of-seven).

sweep:
  term: Sweep
  description: Winning a playoff series 4-0 without opponent winning any games.
seven_game_series:
  term: Seven Game Series
  description: Playoff series that goes to maximum seven games.
season_mvp:
  term: Season MVP
  description: Most Valuable Player award for the NBA Finals series.

conference:
  term: Conference
  description: Eastern or Western Conference grouping.
division:
  term: Division
  description: Sub-conference groupings (Atlantic, Central, Southeast, Northwest, Pacific, Southwest).

field_goals:
  term: Field Goals
  description: Successful shots from the field (excluding free throws).
rebounds:
  term: Rebounds
  description: Retrieved missed shots (offensive + defensive).
assists:
  term: Assists
  description: Passes directly leading to made field goals.
steals:
  term: Steals
  description: Defensive plays resulting in possession change.
blocks:
  term: Blocks
  description: Defensive rejection of opponent's shot attempt.
turnovers:
  term: Turnovers
  description: Loss of possession through error or violation.

PER:
  term: Player Efficiency Rating
  description: Overall player performance metric.
"TS%":
  term: True Shooting Percentage
  description: Shooting efficiency accounting for 2PT, 3PT, and FT.
"USG%":
  term: Usage Rate
  description: Percentage of team possessions used by player.
BPM:
  term: Box Plus/Minus
  description: Player's contribution per 100 possessions.
VORP:
  term: Value Over Replacement Player
  description: Player's total value contribution.
win_shares:
  term: Win Shares
  description: Estimated wins contributed by player.
offensive_rating:
  term: Offensive Rating
  description: Points scored per 100 possessions.
defensive_rating:
  term: Defensive Rating
  description: Points allowed per 100 possessions.
net_rating:
  term: Net Rating
  description: Offensive Rating minus Defensive Rating.
pace:
  term: Pace
  description: Estimated possessions per game.

efficiency:
  term: Efficiency
  description: Statistical metric of productivity relative to usage or opportunity.
usage_rate:
  term: Usage Rate
  description: Percentage of team plays used by a player while on court.
true_shooting:
  term: True Shooting
  description: Shooting efficiency accounting for 2pt, 3pt, and free throws.

starting_lineup:
  term: Starting Lineup
  description: Five players who begin a game on the court.
rotation:
  term: Rotation
  description: Regular pattern of player substitutions during games.
coaching_staff:
  term: Coaching Staff
  description: Coaching staff assignments linking coaches to teams by season, encompassing all coaching roles (head coaches, associate head coaches, lead assistant coaches, assistant coaches, and player development coaches).
head_coach:
  term: Head Coach
  description: Primary coach responsible for team strategy and game management (coach_type = 'Head Coach' in coaching_staff entity).
assistant_coach:
  term: Assistant Coach
  description: Supporting coach who assists the head coach in various capacities (coach_type includes 'Assistant Coach', 'Lead Assistant Coach', 'Associate Head Coach', 'Assistant Coach for Player Development' in coaching_staff entity).

salary_cap:
  term: Salary Cap
  description: Maximum total salary a team can pay its players.
draft_combine:
  term: Draft Combine
  description: Annual event where NBA prospects undergo physical measurements and athletic testing.
draft_position:
  term: Draft Position
  description: Overall pick number in the NBA draft (lower numbers = higher picks).
lottery_pick:
  term: Lottery Pick
  description: First 14 picks in the draft (picks 1-14).
first_round:
  term: First Round
  description: First 30 picks in the draft (round 1).
free_agency:
  term: Free Agency
  description: Period when player contracts expire and players can sign with any team.

streak:
  term: Streak
  description: Consecutive games with same outcome (wins or losses).
standings:
  term: Standings
  description: Ranking of teams by winning percentage within conference/division.
sababa:
  term: sababa game
  description: a sababa game is a game that was broadcasted live on tv
